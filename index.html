About this 3D Experience

This is a lightweight interactive Three.js developer workspace built with basic geometry — no external 3D models or large textures. Drag or touch-drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and click any highlighted object to select it and recolor or re-material it from the panel on the right. The scene loads only once it scrolls into view and pauses automatically when off-screen.
